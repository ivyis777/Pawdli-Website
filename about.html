About Us

Born From Compassion. Built for Every Paw. 🐾

More Than a Pet App.
A Purpose for Every Paw.

PAWdLI was not created just to build another pet app.

It started with a simple but deeply important question: What happens to the pets who have nobody when they die?

Seeing abandoned and deceased pets lying helpless on roads deeply affected us. These animals may have once been someone's beloved companion, yet in their final moments, they are often left without care, dignity, or anyone to say goodbye.

That thought became our purpose.

We wanted to create a way to give orphaned, abandoned and unclaimed pets a respectful and dignified farewell.

And that is where the journey of PAWdLI began.

From One Purpose to an Entire Pet Ecosystem

One Purpose Became
a Bigger Vision

As we started working on the Pet Farewell initiative, we realized something bigger.

Pets are not just animals. They are companions, family members, friends and an important part of our lives.

Pet parents need reliable services. Pet lovers want a community. Pets need homes. Pet businesses need a way to connect with customers. Pet parents need access to veterinarians.

And sometimes, people simply need a place to share their love and stories about pets.

Why not create one place for all of them?

That vision became PAWdLI — a digital ecosystem for pets.

What is PAWdLI?

A Digital Ecosystem
for Pets

PAWdLI is a platform bringing together pet parents, pet lovers, pet businesses, veterinarians, animal welfare communities and pet enthusiasts under one digital ecosystem.

Our goal is simple:

To make life with pets more connected, convenient, meaningful and compassionate.

From discovering pet products to finding a veterinarian, from adopting a pet to making new friends, from listening to pet stories to providing a dignified farewell — PAWdLI brings different parts of the pet journey together.

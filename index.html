WELCOME TO PAWdLI

A Better World for Every Paw. 🐾

Connect. Care. Celebrate. Remember.

PAWdLI is a digital ecosystem created for pet parents, pet lovers, pet businesses, and the people who care about animals.

From finding a new buddy and adopting a pet to meeting a vet, discovering pet products, listening to pet stories, and giving a forgotten pet a dignified farewell — PAWdLI is there for every part of the pet journey.

Because every paw deserves to matter.
OUR STORY

It Started With a Painful Sight.

We often see abandoned and deceased pets lying helpless on the roads, with nobody to care for them or give them a dignified farewell.

That painful reality inspired us to start PAWdLI.

What began as a mission to provide a respectful farewell for orphaned and unclaimed pets evolved into something much bigger — a connected ecosystem for the entire pet community.

Today, PAWdLI brings together people, pets, businesses, veterinarians, stories, care, and compassion — all in one place.

OUR HEART — PET FAREWELL

Because Love Shouldn’t End at Goodbye.

Every pet deserves dignity.

Our Pet Farewell initiative was born from the heartbreaking sight of orphaned and abandoned pets left on roads without anyone to care for them.

We want to change that.

PAWdLI is working towards creating a compassionate system where these forgotten pets can receive the respect, care and dignified farewell they deserve.

No pet should be forgotten.

BUILT AROUND THE HUMAN–PET BOND

Pets Don’t Just Live With Us. They Become Family. ❤️

They make us smile.

They comfort us when we’re down.

They bring families together.

They become our companions, our friends and sometimes our greatest source of emotional support.

PAWdLI exists to celebrate this extraordinary bond — and to build a community that cares for pets throughout their journey.

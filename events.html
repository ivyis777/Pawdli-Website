PAWDL PET EVENTS

Moments We’ve Shared

A collection of the events we attended, the people we met, and the thoughts we carried with us.

EVENT 01

Bengaluru Pet Fed

January 2026

We had the wonderful opportunity to connect with pet parents and pet lovers in Bengaluru and introduce them to Pawdli — a platform created to make pet care, companionship, and pet services more accessible.

Our thoughts: Meeting pet lovers, understanding their needs, and sharing our vision made the event truly special. It was inspiring to see how much love people have for their pets and strengthened our belief in building a caring and connected pet community.

EVENT 02

Malayala Manorama Paw Summit in Kerala

March 2026

We attended the Malayala Manorama Paw Summit in Kerala, organized by Roms N Roks. The event featured exciting dog shows, South India’s first mega grooming competition, grooming masterclasses, an exotic pet park, an interactive Paw Party, and live music.

Our thoughts: It was an exciting experience filled with learning, connection, and plenty of happy tails. Meeting pet lovers, watching skilled groomers, and being part of such a vibrant pet community inspired us to keep creating meaningful experiences for pets and their families. 🐾💛

EVENT 03

Mumbai Pet Fed

March 2026

We were happy to be part of another pet gathering where we could introduce our app and connect directly with the people it is made for.

Our thoughts: Events like this help us see the real needs of pet parents beyond a screen. Their feedback and stories are an important part of our journey.

IN LOVING MEMORY

A Heartfelt Pet Farewell

A Moment to Remember

We gave one beloved pet a peaceful farewell, gently laying them to rest and placing flowers as a final gesture of love and respect.

Our thoughts: This moment reminded us that every pet’s life matters. Even in their final moments, they deserve care, peace, and respect. Placing flowers was our small way of saying thank you for the love and memories they brought into the world. 🐾🌸
